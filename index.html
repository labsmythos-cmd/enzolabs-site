Questions people actually ask

If yours isn't here, call the demo line and ask the agent.

Will my customers know it's not a person?

Some will, some won't. We don't tell it to pretend — it gives your business name and gets to the point. Most callers care that someone picked up at 9pm, not who.

What if it can't answer something?

It takes a message and texts you, or forwards the call to your cell if you want it to. It won't guess at something it doesn't know.

Does it work with my booking system?

Usually. Tell us what you use on the demo call and we'll say yes or no before you pay anything.

What does it cost?

Depends on your call volume, so we quote it after the demo. No setup fee on your first month, and no contract.

What happens to my call recordings?

They're yours. Transcripts go to your inbox, and you can have us delete recordings on any schedule you want.
